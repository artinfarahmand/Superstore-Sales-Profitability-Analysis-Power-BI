# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis – Power BI

## Overview
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing the classic Superstore dataset.  
Focus areas: Sales performance, Profitability, Category/Sub-Category deep dive, Discount impact, and Regional analysis.

## Dashboard Structure

### Page 1 – Executive Overview
- KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Average Order Value, Total Customers, Total Orders, Profit Margin
- Sales by Region (Treemap)
- Sales & Profit Trend over time
- Sales by Category (Bar)
- Top Products table
- Sales by Segment (Donut)

### Page 2 – Category & Sub-Category Analysis
- Interactive filters: Order Date, State, Country
- Sales distribution by Category (Pie)
- Profit by Sub-Category (Horizontal Bar)
- Profit by Category
- Detailed matrix: Category → Sub-Category with Sales, Orders, and Profit Margin

### Page 3 – Profitability Analysis
- KPI cards focused on Profit & Discount
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Scatter plot: Average Discount vs Total Profit (bubble size = Sales) colored by Category
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Region
- Category summary table (Sales, Profit, Margin, Avg Discount)

## Key Business Insights
- Technology is the primary profit driver.
- Furniture (especially Tables & Bookcases) has very low or negative margins despite significant sales.
- High discounts are strongly correlated with lower profitability.
- West region significantly outperforms other regions.
- Consumer segment dominates sales volume.

## Tools & Skills Demonstrated
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX measures
- Data modeling
- Interactive visualizations (Treemap, Scatter, Donut, Matrix, KPI cards)
- Storytelling with data

## How to Use
1. Open the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.
2. Use the page navigation at the bottom.
3. Interact with filters and visuals (especially on Page 2 and the scatter on Page 3).
